Turn on Developer options

One time only, on your watch

Samsung Galaxy Watch

1
Settings → About watch → Software information
2
Tap Software version 7 times.
You see “Developer mode turned on”.
— or —

Google Pixel Watch and most others

1
Settings → System → About → Versions
2
Tap Build number 7 times.

Then, on every watch

3
Settings → Developer options
(new, at the bottom)
4
Turn ADB debugging ON
5
Turn Wireless debugging ON
Watch on the same Wi-Fi as your phone.
Keep Bluetooth ON.
Menu names can vary a little by watch.
